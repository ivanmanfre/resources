Saving us both a discovery call.

Not every business should buy this. If you fall into one of these categories, the math doesn't work yet and I'll tell you so on the call. Probably easier if you self-select out now.

  • Pre-revenue solo consultants. You don't have a content problem yet, you have a client problem. Go book five discovery calls the hard way first, then come back when LinkedIn is your bottleneck not your distraction.

07 • FAQ

The questions I get on every discovery call.

Will it actually sound like me, or will it read like AI slop?

This is the question I get most. The honest answer: it sounds like you if you've shipped enough content for the system to learn from. I train a voice profile from your existing LinkedIn archive, your call transcripts, and a short brand interview during week one. After 10-15 approved posts the model is dialled in, and your edit time on each new draft drops to seconds.

If you've never posted on LinkedIn, the first two weeks are a calibration phase where you'll edit more heavily. That's fine. The system gets sharper as the corpus grows.

What do you need from me to build it?

Concretely: a 30-minute brand interview in week one, access to your LinkedIn archive (just exported posts is fine), your call recordings library if you have one (Fathom, Granola, Fireflies, whatever), and approval rights on the accounts where the system will live.

That's it for week one. After that the system runs on its own intake, you just review the queue.

Can I edit posts before they ship?

Yes, every post. The queue sits in your review tool with the draft, the hook variations, the source material, and the QA score. Edit anything, reject anything, swap hooks, change the publish time. Nothing ships until you approve it.

Some clients edit 20% of drafts heavily for the first month, then edit nothing for months on end as the system calibrates.

What happens after the two weeks? Am I locked in?

No lock-in. The system runs in your accounts, on your stack. Vector database, workspace tool, LinkedIn integration, lead magnet pages, follow-up email service, all in your name. The prompts, agent configs, and templates are documented and editable.

Optional ongoing care plan if you want me to monitor, tune voice, and add features over time. Most clients stay on it for six months then offboard cleanly. A few take the system off-platform and run it themselves on day 15. Your call.

My LinkedIn audience is small. Does this still work?

Lead magnets work harder when your audience is small, because LinkedIn pushes them to non-followers via the algorithm if they engage. Daily posting compounds audience faster than weekly posting, and consistency is the real lever for follower growth at any size.

That said: if your audience is under 500 connections and you've never posted, the lead magnet engine is the bigger near-term win. Posts will compound over months. I'll tell you on the call which to prioritize.

What if I don't like the output after the build?

We work in weekly review cycles during the build. End of week one you see the first drafts, the lead magnet wireframe, and the architecture. If the direction is off, we adjust before the system goes live.

If at the end of two weeks the system genuinely isn't producing usable output for your business, I refund the final invoice. Hasn't happened yet, but the door is on the wall, not the ceiling.

Why does the call transcript matter so much?

Because the best content material you have is already in your client calls. The framework breakthroughs, the war stories, the contrarian takes. They all happen in real conversations and then evaporate. The system indexes those, identifies which moments are post-shaped, and turns them into drafts in your voice.

If you don't record your calls today, week one of the build includes setting that up. Fathom or Granola are both fine.
