01 • The Problem

What’s costing you time

About half the contacts sitting in your pipeline have no working email, so the SDR engine never reaches them at all. CMD flags roughly 788 new companies a month with live projects, and your CRM holds around two people at each one.

Currently absorbing approximately 15+ hours per week in manual work.

02 • The Solution

What we’re building

Reach the estimators and project managers your email engine cannot touch, on LinkedIn, timed to the bid, and turn them into first orders.

Built on n8n. Designed to remove manual touchpoints while keeping you in full control.

System Flow

01
Triggers
CMD flags a new project and the company enters Pipedrive. We filter to companies that have never ordered, then pick the contacts your email engine cannot reach: no email on file, unverified, or previously bounced.
02
Process
We find those people on LinkedIn plus their colleagues on the same project, run a liveness check so we skip dead profiles, and queue a connect. Every message is drafted against the live project and waits for your approval before it sends.
03
Output
A Pipedrive record is created the moment the connect goes out, stamped with the source, the date and the thread. When they reply, the conversation runs until they ask for a quote or book time with your team.
04
Confirm
Your rep inherits a record that already carries the stamp, so nothing depends on anyone remembering to log it. You get a saved filter in your own Pipedrive showing every deal this lane produced, which you can open any time without asking me for a number.
