YOUR FIRST DRAFT
Install Inbound Content Patterns, then open the editor link above. If ChatGPT shows a loading error, select Retry.

Paste your draft and choose AI and automation, Ecommerce or Mobile games. Something else loads a labelled broader sample. Choose Whole post or Opening only, then click Improve my post or Improve the opening.

Your draft stays in the editor. The rewrite appears in the ChatGPT conversation. Open the Examples tab to browse real authors, engagement figures and original post links.

Closing or reloading the editor clears your draft. Save your text before leaving.

FOLLOW-UP REQUESTS
Use these in the ChatGPT conversation after sending a draft from the editor. Replace the brackets with your own details.

ANOTHER VERSION
For the draft I just sent, give me another version for [reader]. Keep the facts I supplied. Explain which changes come from the research and which are your writing choices.

ANOTHER OPENING
Give me another opening for the draft I just sent. Keep the rest of the post unchanged and use only the facts I supplied. Explain the reason for the change.

STUDY AN EXAMPLE
Show me the original links for the examples behind your suggestions. Explain the author-relative engagement comparison and whether each example applies to my topic. Help me use a relevant structure with these facts from my work: [your facts].

WORKED EXAMPLE FROM THE TESTED EDITOR FLOW
Original opening:
I find a ton of content ideas coming from client comms on Slack...

Revised opening:
I get a ton of content ideas from client conversations on Slack.

The body paragraphs stayed unchanged. ChatGPT explained that tightening the wording was an editorial choice. The research does not predict how either version will perform.
