06 • FAQ

The questions I get on every discovery call.

Will it actually sound like me?

Yes, if you've shipped enough content for the system to learn from. Voice profile is trained from your LinkedIn archive, call transcripts, and a short brand interview in week one. After 10-15 approved posts your edit time drops to seconds per draft.

What do you need from me to build it?

A 30-minute brand interview in week one. Access to your LinkedIn archive. Your call recordings if you have them (Fathom, Granola, whatever). Approval rights on the accounts where the system lives. That's it.

Can I edit posts before they ship?

Every one. The queue shows the draft, hook variations, source material, and QA score. Edit, reject, swap hooks, change publish time. Nothing ships without your approval.

Am I locked in after the two weeks?

No. The system runs in your accounts, on your stack. Database, workspace tool, LinkedIn integration, lead magnet pages, all in your name. Optional care plan if you want me to monitor and tune. Most clients stay on it. A few run it themselves from day 15. Your call.

What if I don't like the output?

Weekly reviews during the build catch direction problems early. If at the end of two weeks the system genuinely isn't producing usable output, I refund the final invoice. Hasn't happened yet.
