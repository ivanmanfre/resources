Phase 0 · Pre-call classification (1 min)

Lead temperature determines pitch path

Before any discovery call, tag the lead. This determines which Phase 11 branch you use (everything from Phase 1–10 is identical for all paths).

COLD
Upwork / cold outbound / no prior content exposure.
→ Phase 11a (Blueprint pitch $2,500)
LUKEWARM
Content inbound, has read/watched your stuff, no human referral.
→ Phase 11a (Blueprint pitch $2,500)
WARM
Human referral (Kyle Hunt → Henner/Interlude, existing client → friend).
→ Phase 11b (Fractional pitch $10k+)
HOT
Referred by recently-closed happy client with strong endorsement.
→ Phase 11b (Fractional pitch $10k+)
Phase 1

Opening 60 sec

"Hey, thanks for hopping on. I'm Ivan, founder of Ivan System. On this call I'm going to ask you a bunch of questions about how your business actually runs and where AI could move the needle for you specifically — then I'll walk you through how we work together. Sound good?"

Then ask, in order:

  • What made you book this call?
  • What did you watch / read of mine before booking? What part hit?
  • Is this something you've been thinking about for a while, or is it new this month?
  • Soft DM check: "Quick framing — is this a decision you'd make solo or with a partner / co-founder?" (Just gather info. Don't try to lock a second call yet — that comes later.)

Don't skip these. They're your motivation, anchor, urgency reads, and committee map.

Phase 2

Axis Bifurcation Critical · routes the call

"Quick framing question — when you think about why you're looking at AI right now, is it more about buying back time and cost in the business, or more about getting more output / growing without hiring proportionally? Or honestly, both?"

Route based on answer:

  • Save: run hours × $/hr cost-of-inaction math
  • Gain: run ceiling × time math
  • Both: lead with bigger fire, address other in recap
Phase 3

Current AI / Automation State

"Where are you currently with AI and automation — nowhere, duct-taped some things, or real systems running?"

  • Nothing: "What's been the blocker — time, knowing where to start, trust?"
  • Tried, didn't stick: "What did you try? Where did it break? How much went into it?"
  • Real systems: "If they're working, what made you book?" (forces gap or self-DQ)
Phase 4

Pain Excavation two-track

Track A — SAVE (time/cost buyer)

  • "How involved are you in day-to-day ops?"
  • "What % of your week goes to work an AI agent or system could handle?"
  • "How's that showing up outside work — evenings, weekends, headspace?"

Run the math out loud:

"[X hours] × [$Y founder rate] × 4 weeks = $[Z]/month. Over 12 months: $[annual]. Sound right?"

"What happens if nothing changes in 12 months?"

Track B — GAIN (growth/output buyer)

  • "Where's the ceiling? When you push more output, what breaks first — people, process, systems?"
  • "If you doubled output tomorrow, what would have to be true?"
  • "Last time you turned down work or delayed a launch because of capacity — what did that cost?"

Run the math out loud:

"You're at [current output]. If the ceiling moved to [target], that's [delta] in additional revenue/leverage per month. Over 12 months: [$ or units]. The longer the ceiling stays, your competitors compound past you."

"What happens if the ceiling stays where it is for 12 months?"

Phase 5

Team Structure & Agent-Readiness

  • "How's the team structured? Who reports to who?"
  • Agent-readiness: "If I dropped an AI agent into your business tomorrow, where would it have clean data and process to act on — and where would it hit a wall?"
  • Capacity test: "If I sent you 5 perfect-fit clients tomorrow, could you take them on, or would it break the team?"
Phase 6

Past Attempts

  • "What have you tried before — tools, hires, courses, agencies?"
  • For each: "Why didn't it stick?"
  • "Roughly how much have you spent — money, hours, headspace?"
  • "Why do you think working with me would be different?" (prospect-sells-themselves)
Phase 7

Desired Outcome

Track A

  • "If we got this right, what would you have back? Hours? Headspace?"
  • "What would you do with [X hours] back?"

Track B

  • "If we got this right, what would you be doing more of?"
  • "From [current] to what specifically?"

Both tracks: "What do you NOT want anymore?"

Phase 8

Decision-Maker Lock-In

Now that they've named pain, outcome, and a number, asking about partner alignment feels collaborative — not gating.

If they have a partner / co-founder / ops lead and that person isn't on:

"Earlier you mentioned [name] is part of decisions on this. Based on everything we just talked about, what I'd suggest — let's get [name] on a 20-min call so I can walk them through what we discussed and answer their questions. Would that be a problem?"

If solo: confirm and continue.

Phase 9

Timeline & Trigger Event Do not skip

  • "What timeline are you thinking?"
  • "Why now? Why this month versus six months ago?"

Probe

  • Save triggers: burnout, lost key person, hire too expensive, vacation impossible
  • Gain triggers: turned down contract, competitor shipped AI feature, plateaued revenue, hired without output gain, peer doing 10x
  • AI-curious: board pressure, FOMO, hit ChatGPT wall

If "later":

"When's the last time things calmed down for more than a week?"

"The thing you're waiting to finish — what is it? After that, what's next?"

"Founders who wait for the right time are still waiting two years later. The work happens while it's busy."

Phase 10

Quantified Transition

Track A

"So you're spending [X hrs/week] on work that shouldn't be on your plate — about $[Z]/month, $[annual]/year. Team can't scale past current load. You want [hours back / outcome]. Trigger is [trigger]. What did I miss?"

Track B

"So you're capped at [current output], turning down or delaying [examples], ceiling costing [delta]/month. You want to be at [target] in 12 months. Trigger is [trigger]. What did I miss?"

"Cool. Would it be a problem if I share my screen?"

→ COLD or LUKEWARM lead path
Phase 11a

Pitch — Agent-Ready Blueprint ($2,500)

Variant A — SAVE axis (drowning, time/cost pain)

"Here's what I recommend. You've named [X hrs/week] going to work that shouldn't be on your plate — that's $[Z]/month leaking. The Agent-Ready Blueprint is a 1-week diagnostic that gives you:

1. The 60/25/15 map — what an agent runs, what's augmented, what stays human
2. Prioritized 90-day roadmap ordered by HOURS RECLAIMED
3. 3–5 quick wins — the highest-leverage hours back, first
4. Clear answer on engagement fit

Investment is $2,500, 100% credited toward any engagement. Money-back guarantee if you don't think it was worth it."

Variant B — GAIN axis (ceiling-hitter, growth/output pain)

"Here's what I recommend. You're capped at [current output], turning down [examples] — that ceiling is costing $[delta]/month in forfeited compounding. The Agent-Ready Blueprint is a 1-week diagnostic that gives you:

1. The 60/25/15 map — what an agent runs to break the ceiling, what augments human capacity, what stays human
2. Prioritized 90-day roadmap ordered by OUTPUT UNLOCKED
3. 3–5 quick wins — the fastest path to higher throughput, first
4. Clear answer on which engagement removes the constraint

Investment is $2,500, 100% credited toward any engagement. Money-back guarantee if you don't think it was worth it."

Pick the variant matching the axis answer from Phase 2. If they answered "both", lead with the bigger fire and reference the other in the recap.

Honest-funnel disarm (Blueprint pitch only)
"And I'll be straight — my evil plan is you'll see the roadmap, realize it's a lot of work, and want me to help you do it. That's how this works. But you're under zero obligation, and a chunk of people take the roadmap and run it themselves."
→ WARM or HOT lead path
Phase 11b

Pitch — Fractional m1 ($10k+, skip Blueprint as standalone)

For warm referrals (Kyle Hunt → Henner/Interlude pattern, existing client referral, etc.), the Blueprint as standalone is overkill. Pitch Fractional m1 directly with the 90-Day Blueprint audit INCLUDED as a m1 deliverable.

"Here's what I recommend. Based on what you've shared, going straight into a Fractional engagement makes more sense than starting with a standalone diagnostic. The audit becomes one of the deliverables, not a separate purchase.

Month 1 Fractional — $10,000. I build out your priority systems (LM engine, post engine, newsletter, custom extras — whatever surfaces as P0 from our intake) AND deliver a full 90-Day Blueprint audit that's yours regardless of whether you continue past month 1.

The audit surfaces 5-7 priority builds across content, outbound, production, and partnerships — it's your roadmap, owned by you. We walk through it together in a kickoff + audit call within the first week, then build sprints for 2-3 weeks.

Continuation is opt-in. Most clients continue with an M2+3 Bundle ($12k, ~8% off) to execute the audit's priorities. But if we don't find enough to justify continuation, I'll tell you straight — you graduate to a Care Plan at $1k/mo or just walk. I'd rather lose a month of revenue than ship a padded roadmap.

Four total tiers across the full engagement lifecycle:

• M1 Build: $10k (heavy month, audit + core systems)
• M2-3 Active: $6.5k/mo (or $12k bundle for both — recommended)
• M4+ Slow Lane: $3.5k/mo (lighter cadence, occasional builds)
• Care Plan: $1k/mo (maintenance, no new builds)

Each tier is a mode shift, not a discount. You move down the ladder as the engagement matures."

Single-project downsell (if buyer pushes back on Fractional)
"If you just want one thing built — say the lead magnet system — that's available as a Single Project at $7.5k, no engagement. But you lose the audit, the strategic component, and the ongoing tuning. Most warm referrals find that's a worse deal for the money."
Pace-to-absorption (both pitches)
"One thing I want to flag now: if we end up working together past m1, I pace to your team's absorption — not my delivery speed. The bottleneck on AI in service businesses isn't the build, it's whether your team has the headspace to integrate it. Each month we ship only what you can actually absorb."
Soft scarcity (both, not coercive)
"I onboard a limited number of engagements each quarter. If you want Q3 impact, you'd want to start in the next 2–3 weeks."
Phase 12

Close

For Phase 11a (Blueprint pitch)

"Does this feel like the right next step, or do you need to bring [partner] in first?"

If "let me think":

"Totally fair. What specifically? Sometimes I can answer it right now."

If partner alignment:

"Cool. What's your decision process from here? When and what would they need to hear?"

"Let's lock the follow-up call now — what's [day] look like?"

For Phase 11b (Fractional pitch)

"Want to start with M1? Standard onboarding sequence:

• Discovery done today (we're here now)
• You get the intake link in the next 24 hours — 15-25 min conversation (voice or text, your call)
• Kickoff + audit walkthrough call within the first week
• Builds start shipping in days
• 90-Day Plan Review call at week 3-4 — that's when we look at the audit findings together and decide on the M2+3 Bundle. No surprises, it's already on the calendar from day one.

Want me to send the engagement letter + payment link?"

If "let me think":

"Totally fair. What specifically? Sometimes I can answer it right now. The audit is yours regardless, so the only commitment is the m1 build — which I refund if I don't deliver."

Watch For

Failure modes (Kyle-on-Dutka + v4 additions)

  1. Don't skip "why now" → deal drifts to "let me think about it"
  2. Always run cost/ceiling math out loud → numbers in their words = visceral
  3. Ask the personal-life or competitor-pressure question → keeps it from staying intellectual
  4. Get them to answer "why would this work with me?" → sells themselves
  5. Quantify the summary → generic recap = soft close
  6. Lock follow-up on the call → never end with "I'll email you to schedule"
  7. Match pitch variant to axis → don't pitch hours back to a ceiling-hitter
  8. Match pitch path to lead temperature → don't pitch $2,500 Blueprint to a warm referral; don't pitch $10k Fractional to a cold Upwork lead
  9. Audit-as-deliverable framing matters → DON'T promise the audit will surface m2-3 work. Promise it will be honest. The "I'd rather lose a month than ship a padded roadmap" line IS the brand differentiator.
  10. Don't auto-discount on Fractional pricing pushback → Single Project ($7.5k) is the downsell for buyers who genuinely want one thing. Don't drop the $10k m1 rate. Holding the anchor protects future deals.
  11. 4-tier ladder visibility builds trust → show all four tiers explicitly during Fractional pitch. Signals "this is a real engagement structure, not a contract you're trapped in."
  12. Voice vs text intake choice → buyer picks at intake start. Don't pre-decide for them. (Voice intake mode rolls out via Sub-Project A — until then, text-only.)
