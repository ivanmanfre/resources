## Fit

## Opening draft

Missing research question: does this campaign already have a dedicated landing page? The founder has selected this account for an exploratory question. These drafts carry no confirmed purchase need.

Hey Maya, I saw Harbor's campaign focuses on customer retention. We build landing-page sprints for B2B analytics companies around one use case. The page gives the campaign somewhere to explain the use case and the next step for the buyer. Have you already got a dedicated page for that campaign?

Evidence: the campaign fact comes from S1; the service comes from the example agency offer. There is no claim about Harbor's conversion rate or budget.

## Follow-up draft

Hey Maya, following up on the customer-retention campaign. If you've already got the page, I'd be interested to hear what it needs to explain. Is it focused on a particular kind of subscription business?

Timing: five business days after the opening message, subject to a fresh thread and contact-status check.

## Reply triage

“What would your sprint change?” → needs information. Ask for the page and the campaign's intended next step before proposing changes. Draft: “Could you send the page and tell me what you want visitors to do after reading it? I can explain the sprint against that.”

“Please remove me from your list.” → opt-out, do_not_contact: true, account cedar-002. Next action: add the account to the do-not-contact record and remove its pending follow-ups. Optional acknowledgement draft: “Understood, I'll remove you from the list.”

“Thanks.” → unclear. The reply acknowledges the message and supplies no buying intent.
