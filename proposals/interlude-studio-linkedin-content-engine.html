03 • Scope of Work

What’s included

Content System
Post pipeline · 5 post types drafted daily from your calls and research. 10 hook variations per post, one tap to approve from Airtable.
Lead Magnet engine · 5 formats generated from approved post topics. Each asset comes with a landing page, resource page, cover image and email draft — ready to publish.
Content Brain · the system gets smarter with every call you take. Past conversations feed directly into post quality and positioning over time.
Source pipeline · call transcripts, manual seeds, and competitor repurposing fed in automatically. No manual briefing needed.
Voice profile · trained from your existing LinkedIn archive. Posts read like you wrote them, not like a template. AI-ism QA agent strips template language before anything reaches the queue.
Workspace
Airtable workspace · review queue, published log and prompt library in one place. Tweak tone and style in-house without touching the workflow.
LinkedIn scheduler · approved posts go straight to LinkedIn on schedule. Lead magnet DM distribution wired in.
Email capture · lead magnet signups flow into ConvertKit automatically. Lists configured and ready for newsletters when you want them.
Lead magnet pages · each asset gets a branded landing page and resource page on resources.interlude.studio. Generated automatically, no design work required.
In-browser LM editor · branded interface where your team edits LM copy directly, autosaves, and publishes to the live URL. No code, no Ivan dependency for text updates. Magic-link auth so only Interlude folks can edit Interlude LMs.
Handoff docs · full runbook delivered Day 14. How everything runs, how to tweak it, how to expand it.
