# Your agency offer

Replace the bracketed fields with your own details. Leave a field marked unknown when you do not have evidence.

- Agency name: [name]
- Service: [the work clients buy]
- Buyer role: [who can buy it]
- Target companies: [company type and useful size or stage signals]
- Relevant problem: [the specific problem your service addresses]
- Engagement: [retainer, sprint or project]
- Price information approved for drafts: [amount or omit pricing]
- Approved proof: [exact claim, its source and permission to share]
- Exclusions: [companies or situations you would decline]
- First conversation: [one question you want to resolve]
- Tone example: [a message you wrote yourself]
- Follow-up timing: [your chosen interval and maximum follow-ups]
- Do-not-contact records: [identifiers, or none recorded]

Only claims entered in Approved proof can appear as results in a message. An empty proof field means the message describes the offer and asks a relevant question.
