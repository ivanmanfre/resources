Your stack (how the pieces fit)

Four pieces, cloud-first. You told me nothing you handle is truly sensitive, so we keep it simple and don't over-engineer:

  • Claude Code — your cockpit. You ask it things, it builds, it runs your skills.
  • n8n — the automation layer. Runs your flows on a schedule or a trigger, no babysitting.
  • Apollo + Pipedrive — your pipeline. Leads in, enriched, every touch tracked in one place.
  • Slack — where your reports land.
The Mac Studio (arriving ~July) is a bonus, not a dependency. Because your data isn't sensitive, we go cloud-first. The Mac Studio just makes the heavy, repetitive jobs cheaper down the line. We don't build around it.
