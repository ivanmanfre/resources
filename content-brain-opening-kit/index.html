What the numbers measure.

The October 16 study used 17,322 public posts from 217 authors across four research cohorts. It selected 1,709 same-author, same-format high/low pairs. High posts had at least 2× the author baseline; low posts had at most 0.7×. The work-only check retained 828 pairs. Previews used the first three lines, capped at 210 characters, with media hidden.

The outcome was log(1 + likes + 3 × reposts), adjusted for age and compared with the author’s nearby same-format median. Topic and image effects can remain. The figures describe observed engagement; they do not measure see-more clicks, leads or a new draft’s success rate.

The October 9 study separately used 10,629 posts from 140 authors in three niches. Its sample overlaps with the October 16 study, so the counts stay separate. The rewrite and choice of examples in this kit are editorial work.
