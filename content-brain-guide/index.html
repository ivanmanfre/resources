What's behind the research? +
10,629 posts · 140 authors

The October 9 writing study compared posts with their own author's baseline across three niches. Author-voiced openings and story-carried bodies had positive associations in parts of the sample. A work-only follow-up weakened broad advice to add personal moments.

17,322 posts · separate hook study

The October 16 hook study examined four research cohorts. Selected peer comparisons did not reliably rank Ivan's own posts. The samples overlap; the counts aren't added together.

These are observed associations. They don't establish a formula for future engagement or buyer conversions. The research compares likes plus three times reposts against an author's median. The simplified likes example above explains the idea.
