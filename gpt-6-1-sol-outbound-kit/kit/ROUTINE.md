# Morning and weekly routine

## Each morning

Start with replies. Run reply triage on each new thread. Record opt-outs first, resolve information requests and keep the prospect's agreed dates beside the account ID.

Open the follow-up queue. Review the latest thread before preparing the next draft. Remove accounts with a reply, a refusal or a do-not-contact record. Keep timing tied to your own stated interval.

Choose the accounts you want to research next. Run account research, then fit. Resolve the unknowns that affect the decision before asking for a message draft. Keep the source labels with the account so they survive the next review.

## End of the week

Fill in context/campaign.csv from your sending and reply records. Use a stated first-contact cohort and count unique accounts. Run campaign review and check its arithmetic against the CSV.

Choose one proposed test for the next cohort. Record the message version, the changed variable and the reporting window. Keep unchanged variables visible so the comparison has a usable explanation.

## A useful working queue

| Account ID | Status | Last action | Next action | Date | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ID] | [research / investigate / selected / awaiting reply / follow-up due / closed / do not contact] | [what happened] | [what to do] | [date or unset] | [record] |

The kit returns documents and drafts. Apply the chosen actions to your own records after checking them.
