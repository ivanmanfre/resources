02 • The Solution

What we’re building

Daily LinkedIn posts in your voice, produced from real source signals (calls, competitor activity, DTC news, manual seeds) in under one hour of weekly effort, paired with a 5-format lead magnet engine that captures and qualifies DTC brand leads.

Built on n8n + Anthropic Claude, integrated with Airtable (workspace + prompts + review queue), n8n (orchestration), Anthropic Claude (model), Slack (notifications), Fireflies or your meeting tool (transcript source), ConvertKit (email list). Designed to remove manual touchpoints while keeping you in full control.
