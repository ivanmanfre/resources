02 • The Solution

What we’re building

Daily LinkedIn posts in Henner's voice produced from real source signals (call transcripts, competitor activity, DTC marketing news, manual seeds) in under one hour of weekly effort, paired with a 5-format lead magnet engine that captures and qualifies DTC brand leads.

Built on n8n + Anthropic Claude, integrated with Airtable (workspace + prompt store + review queue), n8n (orchestration), Anthropic Claude (model), Slack (notifications + async requests), Fireflies or your meeting tool (transcript source). Designed to remove manual touchpoints while keeping you in full control.

System Flow

01
Triggers
The system pulls topic ideas automatically from four weighted sources: Fireflies / Granola call transcripts (weight 3.0 = highest signal), manual seeds Henner drops into Airtable (weight 2.0), competitor DTC agency posts and viral DTC ads (weight 0.5), and DTC marketing news and trend research (weight 0.3). Lead magnet creation is triggered manually when Henner picks a topic cluster.
02
Process
Post Generation pipeline runs 5 sub-agents in sequence: Topic Suggester proposes angles from the weighted source pool, Strategy Scorer ranks them against ICP fit and editorial pillars, Hook Generator produces 3 to 5 hook variants per approved topic, Copywriter writes the post in Henner's voice, QA Editor enforces editorial rules and forbidden-language filters. In parallel, the Lead Magnet engine produces capture assets in 5 formats (Interactive Assessment, Guide, Template, Checklist, Swipe File) from approved topic clusters.
03
Output
Ready-to-publish LinkedIn posts and live lead magnet URLs land in the Airtable review board with hook variants, CTAs, source attribution, and recommended publish slots
04
Confirm
Slack ping per batch, one tap in Airtable to approve and push live
