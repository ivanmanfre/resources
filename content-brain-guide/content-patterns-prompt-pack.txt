SETUP
Install from the listing. Start a new chat, type @ and select Inbound Content Patterns. Replace the brackets in a prompt below with your own niche and facts.

REWRITE MY DRAFT
My niche is [AI and automation / ecommerce / mobile games marketing]. Use Inbound Content Patterns to find relevant patterns and source posts for this draft. Explain which changes the research supports and which are your writing judgment. Rewrite it using only my facts and keep my voice. Draft: [paste your draft]

COMPARE TWO IDEAS
I write for [reader] in [AI and automation / ecommerce / mobile games marketing]. Use Inbound Content Patterns to compare these two ideas: [idea A] and [idea B]. Show the closest public examples and explain which is better supported. Tell me when the match is weak. Help me choose an angle using these facts: [your facts].

FIND STANDOUT POSTS
Use Inbound Content Patterns to find posts in [AI and automation / ecommerce / mobile games marketing] that beat their own author's usual engagement. Show the original links, the author-relative comparison and what I can study in each opening. Help me apply a relevant pattern to this real experience: [your experience].

WORKED EXAMPLE
Demo draft:
Daily sales reporting is easy to automate with n8n. My workflow pulls orders, groups sales by product and emails me the summary.

Demo rewrite:
I built a daily sales report that lands in my inbox.

It runs in n8n:
- Pull orders
- Group sales by product
- Email the daily summary

Now I can see what sold in one email.

The opening applies the author-voiced pattern found in the automation study. Separating the steps is an editorial choice. This synthetic example keeps the draft's facts and includes no promised performance improvement.
