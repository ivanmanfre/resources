01 • The honest read

The math on LinkedIn doesn't pencil out when you do it by hand.

Most agency owners I talk to know LinkedIn would print money for them. They've seen peers do it. They've tried themselves. The reason they're not consistent isn't motivation. It's that the time cost is brutal and the work doesn't compound when you do it manually. The usual story:

  • Each post takes an hour if you want it good. Researching the angle, drafting it, editing for voice, formatting. Five posts a week is a part-time job nobody on your team wants.
  • Lead magnets take a full week to build, and most never ship because by day four you remember you have an agency to run.
  • Your last lead magnet got fifty downloads and zero calls because nothing followed up automatically and you forgot to chase them manually.
