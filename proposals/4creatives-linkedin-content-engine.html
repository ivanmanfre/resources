02 • The Solution

What we’re building

Daily LinkedIn posts in Henner's voice produced from briefs, call transcripts, or topic seeds in under one hour of weekly effort, paired with a lead magnet engine that captures and qualifies DTC brand leads from that audience.

Built on n8n + Anthropic Claude, integrated with Airtable, Notion, ClickUp, ChatGPT (Zapier-based current attempt). Designed to remove manual touchpoints while keeping you in full control.
